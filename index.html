About ZENKAI

Turning ideas into applications.

We envision a world where building software is simpler, more accessible, and less time-consuming.

Technology should empower ideas.

ZENKAI is being designed to help people and businesses create applications through visual development tools, connected data and automated workflows.

The people behind it

Built by real people, for real ideas.

ZENKAI started with a simple belief: building software should be within reach for the people who have the ideas, not only the people who write code.

Founder & CEO

Arjun has spent over a decade building business software and watching capable teams get stuck waiting on developers for small changes. He started ZENKAI to close that gap — giving the people closest to a problem the tools to build the solution themselves.

“I built ZENKAI for people with great ideas and no easy way to ship them. If that's you, I can't wait to see what you make.”
The story behind ZENKAI

Why ZENKAI exists.

Turning an idea into working software is still harder than it should be. ZENKAI is an attempt to change that.

The problem

Building software usually means writing code, managing data, and wiring up workflows by hand — slow work that needs specialised skills.

What makes it different

ZENKAI brings visual development, connected data, automated workflows and controlled access together in one platform, so you can build without the complexity.

The approach

Design visually first, connect your data, automate the repetitive parts, and keep access controlled — all from one place.

The long-term vision

A world where building software is simpler, more accessible and less time-consuming — helping more people turn ideas into useful digital solutions.

Sample content  ZENKAI began with a familiar frustration: teams that knew exactly what they needed, but had to wait weeks for a simple screen or report. We wanted to put that power back in their hands — so building an application feels less like coding and more like designing.
